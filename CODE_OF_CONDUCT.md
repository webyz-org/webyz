# Code of conduct

Webyz is a small project and we want it to stay a pleasant place to work. Everyone who participates, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, agrees to the following.

**Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good faith. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks and unwelcome sexual attention are not tolerated, whoever they come from.

**Be constructive.** Explain what and why. Reviews point at the code, propose alternatives, and accept that the maintainers make the final call on direction.

**Be considerate of time.** Maintainers and contributors are volunteers or working on this alongside other jobs. A slow reply is not a snub.

**Respect privacy.** Do not post other people's private information, and do not use real customer data in issues, examples or tests.

If someone behaves in a way that breaks these rules, report it to the address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) with "conduct" in the subject. Reports are handled privately by the maintainers. Depending on severity, the response ranges from a private word to removal from the project's spaces.
